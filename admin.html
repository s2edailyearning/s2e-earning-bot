ℹ️ How ROI works

• ROI starts on the member\'s Activation date (Profile → Activation).
• Runs for Total Days from activation (e.g. 50 days).
• Total per member = Daily Amount × Total Days (e.g. ₹40 × 50 = ₹2,000).
• Example: set Daily = 20, Days = 100 for the "20 × 100 days" plan.

GRANT SELECT,INSERT,UPDATE ON store_inventory TO anon,authenticated; GRANT SELECT,INSERT ON store_stock_history TO anon,authenticated;

DO $$ BEGIN IF NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM pg_policies WHERE schemaname='public' AND tablename='store_inventory' AND policyname='flm_store_inventory_anon_all') THEN CREATE POLICY flm_store_inventory_anon_all ON store_inventory FOR ALL TO anon,authenticated USING (true) WITH CHECK (true); END IF; END $$;
